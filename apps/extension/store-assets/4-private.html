Privacy

No server. No account.

The extension talks to exactly one thing: the Vortex app on your own computer, over a local channel. There is nowhere else for anything to go.

No telemetry No analytics, no crash reporting, no usage statistics.
No third parties Nothing is sold or shared, because there is nobody to share it with.
Credentials stay put Cookies travel to your own machine for the download you asked for, and are never written to disk or to a log.
Open source Every line, including this extension. Read it before you trust it.
